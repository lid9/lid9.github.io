About

I'm a student researcher interested in how computational methods — remote sensing, machine learning, spatial analysis — can help answer real environmental questions, from wetland change to urban tree canopy loss. I currently work in the SES Lab at UC Davis, and I'm building a body of independent GIS and data science projects alongside that work.

My background spans R, Python, and GIS (QGIS / ArcGIS), and I'm expanding into raster analysis, image segmentation and classification, and applied deep learning for environmental monitoring.
